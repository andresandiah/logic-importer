The helper is installed. Go back to Logic Importer in LUNA and click Yes.

Where it is:
/Library/Application Support/Logic Importer
/Library/LaunchAgents/com.logicimporter.helper.plist (starts it at login)

To uninstall: in Finder choose Go > Go to Folder, enter /Library/Application Support/Logic Importer, and double-click Uninstall Logic Importer Helper.

The first time you choose a project, macOS may ask to let the helper access your files. Allow it.
